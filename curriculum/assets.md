# Asset Classes — curriculum spec

**End state:** go one level deeper than Investment Fundamentals Module 2 within equities and fixed income, and cover
alternative, international and inflation-linked assets. **Status:** Module 0 is a deep pass.

## Module 0 · Foundations  (`assets-0.html`)
| id | Lesson | Must understand | Real-world link |
|---|---|---|---|
| equity-styles | Equity styles | Growth vs. value; large- vs. small-cap, and how their typical multiples differ | Growth-heavy vs. value-heavy index funds |
| fixed-income-types | Fixed-income types | Government, corporate, municipal and high-yield bonds differ mainly in who owes the money and how risky that is | The yield spread between a government bond and a corporate bond of similar maturity |
| alternative-assets | Alternative assets | Private equity, hedge funds and collectibles trade liquidity and transparency for potentially different return drivers | A "2 and 20" private fund fee structure |
| international-assets | International assets | Developed vs. emerging markets; currency movements add a return component on top of the local return | A US investor's return on a foreign stock depends on both the stock and the exchange rate |
| inflation-protected-assets | Inflation-protected assets | An instrument whose principal or payment adjusts with inflation behaves differently from a fixed-coupon bond | Inflation-linked government bonds (e.g. TIPS-style securities) |
