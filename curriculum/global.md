# Global & Current Events — curriculum spec

**End state:** read macro news, earnings season and geopolitical events as an investor, and tell signal apart from
noise in real time. **Status:** Module 0 is a deep pass.

## Module 0 · Foundations  (`global-0.html`)
| id | Lesson | Must understand | Real-world link |
|---|---|---|---|
| reading-macro-news | Reading macro news | Markets react to the *surprise* relative to expectations, not just the reported number itself | A CPI report that beats or misses economist forecasts |
| earnings-season | Earnings season | Comparing actual results to consensus estimates; an "earnings beat" is relative, not absolute | Quarterly earnings reports moving individual stocks and sometimes whole sectors |
| geopolitics-and-markets | Geopolitics & markets | Political risk can add a required-return premium or move specific sectors and currencies | Energy and defense sectors reacting to geopolitical events |
| currency-and-fx-basics | Currency & FX basics | Exchange rates convert returns between currencies; interest-rate differentials relate to currency carry | Converting a foreign investment's return back to a home currency |
| signal-vs-noise | Signal vs. noise | Most daily headlines are noise relative to a long-term plan; a disciplined process (Investment Fundamentals Module 4) filters it | Reacting to every headline vs. a written, scheduled review process |
