# Investment Strategies — curriculum spec

**End state:** describe value, growth, momentum, income and passive indexing as distinct, repeatable processes rather
than vague labels. **Status:** Module 0 is a basic pass.

## Module 0 · Foundations  (`strategies-0.html`)
| id | Lesson | Must understand | Real-world link |
|---|---|---|---|
| value-investing | Value investing | Buying below estimated intrinsic value, with a "margin of safety" as a buffer against error | Classic value-investing commentary on buying "a dollar for fifty cents" |
| growth-investing | Growth investing | Paying a higher multiple for expected faster earnings growth; the PEG ratio relates P/E to growth | Growth-oriented funds concentrated in fast-expanding industries |
| momentum-investing | Momentum investing | Buying what has recently trended up, on the evidence that trends have historically tended to persist for a time | Momentum factor funds and trend-following strategies |
| income-investing | Income investing | Prioritising current cash income (dividends, coupons) and "yield on cost" as a holding period grows | Dividend-growth portfolios aimed at retirees |
| passive-indexing | Passive indexing | Matching a benchmark cheaply instead of trying to beat it, relying on the market-efficiency and cost arguments from earlier lessons | Broad-market index funds and ETFs |
