# Technical Analysis — curriculum spec

**End state:** read a price chart, understand moving averages, momentum and volume at a beginner level, and know the
evidence for and against technical analysis. **Status:** Module 0 is a basic pass; extends Investment Fundamentals
Lesson 3.5's brief intro to chart reading.

## Module 0 · Foundations  (`technical-0.html`)
| id | Lesson | Must understand | Real-world link |
|---|---|---|---|
| chart-types | Chart types | Line, bar and candlestick charts show the same prices with different amounts of detail | Candlestick charts on virtually every trading platform |
| moving-averages | Moving averages | A moving average smooths price into a trend line; a crossover of two averages is a common (debated) signal | 50-day/200-day moving-average crossovers discussed in market commentary |
| momentum-indicators | Momentum indicators | The relative strength index (RSI) compares the size of recent gains to recent losses | RSI commonly shown under a price chart as an "overbought/oversold" gauge |
| volume-analysis | Volume analysis | Volume confirms or questions a price move — a move on heavy volume is viewed differently from one on thin volume | "Breakout on high volume" as a commonly cited pattern |
| the-ta-debate | The debate around technical analysis | Technical analysis has enthusiastic practitioners and serious academic skeptics; neither view should be taken as settled | Market-efficiency research (Lesson "market-efficiency" in Financial Markets) questions whether patterns are exploitable |
