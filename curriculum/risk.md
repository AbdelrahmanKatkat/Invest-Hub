# Risk & Derivatives — curriculum spec

**End state:** measure drawdown, understand options and futures at a beginner level, and see how derivatives are used
to hedge rather than only to speculate. **Status:** Module 0 is a basic pass.

## Module 0 · Foundations  (`risk-0.html`)
| id | Lesson | Must understand | Real-world link |
|---|---|---|---|
| drawdown-and-var | Drawdown & VaR | Maximum drawdown measures the worst peak-to-trough loss; Value-at-Risk estimates a loss threshold at a given confidence level | Fund fact sheets reporting "maximum drawdown" |
| options-basics | Options basics | A call option's payoff; premium paid is the maximum loss for a buyer | A call option used to bet on or participate in a stock's upside |
| futures-basics | Futures basics | A futures contract controls a large notional value with a much smaller margin deposit — leverage | Commodity and index futures used by institutions and speculators |
| hedging-with-derivatives | Hedging with derivatives | A put option (or a short futures position) can offset losses in an existing holding, like insurance | Portfolio managers buying index puts ahead of an uncertain event |
| the-greeks-intro | The Greeks, briefly | Delta approximates how much an option's price moves per $1 move in the underlying | Delta quoted alongside every listed option |
