# Fundamental Analysis — curriculum spec

**End state:** go beyond Investment Fundamentals Module 3's balance sheet/income statement/multiples/DCF basics into
cash flow, ratio analysis, moats, red flags and qualitative judgement. **Status:** Module 0 is a deep pass.

## Module 0 · Foundations  (`fundamental-0.html`)
| id | Lesson | Must understand | Real-world link |
|---|---|---|---|
| cash-flow-statement | Reading a cash flow statement | Operating, investing and financing cash flow; why reported profit and actual cash can differ (e.g. depreciation) | A profitable company that still runs short of cash |
| ratio-analysis | Ratio analysis | Return on equity (ROE) and return on assets (ROA) as efficiency measures | Comparing ROE across companies in the same industry |
| economic-moats | Economic moats | A durable competitive advantage (brand, network effect, switching costs, scale) that protects long-run profits | Companies commonly cited as having wide "moats" |
| red-flags-in-financials | Red flags in financials | Warning signs such as receivables growing much faster than revenue, or recurring "one-time" charges | Analysts flagging aggressive revenue recognition |
| qualitative-analysis | Qualitative analysis | Management quality, industry position and governance matter alongside the numbers | Management track record discussed in earnings calls |
