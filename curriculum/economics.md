# Economics (Macro & Micro) — curriculum spec

**End state:** explain supply and demand, inflation, interest rates, GDP/growth and the business cycle well enough to
read macro news with understanding. **Status:** Module 0 is a deep pass (full worked examples and depth, same structural standard as every other lesson on
this site). Further modules are not yet planned.

## Module 0 · Foundations  (`economics-0.html`)
| id | Lesson | Must understand | Real-world link |
|---|---|---|---|
| supply-and-demand | Supply & demand | Equilibrium price/quantity; a shift of a curve vs. a movement along it | Oil price moves on OPEC supply decisions vs. seasonal demand changes |
| inflation | Inflation | CPI as a basket-of-goods measure; how inflation erodes purchasing power | Central bank inflation targets; wage and bond negotiations referencing CPI |
| interest-rates | Interest rates | The policy rate as the price of borrowing money; how it ripples through loans, savings and asset prices | A central bank rate hike raising mortgage and savings rates |
| gdp-and-growth | GDP & growth | GDP as total output; real vs. nominal growth; a recession's common definition | Quarterly GDP releases reported in financial news |
| business-cycle | The business cycle | Expansion, peak, contraction, trough; leading vs. lagging indicators | Unemployment claims and PMI surveys used to gauge where the cycle stands |
