How to learn from this

Go in order the first time: each lesson only uses ideas from the ones before it. Inside a lesson the rhythm is always the same:

  1. The question — a concrete problem that makes the idea necessary.
  2. Intuition — the idea in plain words, before any formula.
  3. Worked example — real numbers, computed and checked, then the formal definition with every symbol explained.
  4. Lab — change numbers and watch the result respond. Following the "try this" steps is the fastest way to understand.
  5. Common traps, where it shows up in real investing, and three check yourself questions.
This path is a full deep pass. Every lesson has multiple worked examples, richer labs and extended explanations — the same depth standard Mathematics reached in the sibling AI-Hub project.
Not investment advice. Everything here is general education, with illustrative or historical figures clearly labelled. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any specific security.
